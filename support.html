How do I delete my account?
Open the app, go to Profile → Settings, and choose "Delete account." This permanently removes your account and everything attached to it.
I lost my streak / my results disappeared.
If you played without an account, your streak lives only on that device — reinstalling the app or switching phones starts a new one. Creating an account (Settings → add an account) keeps your streak if that ever happens again.
Why can't I play today's set again, or an old one?
There's one set per day, the same for everyone, and each account plays it once. New players can catch up on the last couple of missed days; after that, each day opens once and stays closed.
I found a bug, or a question seems wrong.
Email us with the day number (shown at the top of the results screen, e.g. "Quintet 37") and what you saw — that's enough for us to find it.
